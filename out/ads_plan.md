# Amazon Ads starter structure

- **SP-Auto**: $4.00/day
- **SP-Manual-Keywords**: $4.00/day
- **SP-Product-Targeting**: $2.00/day

Weekly: move converting search terms from Auto into Manual exact; add non-converting terms (high clicks, no sales) as negatives. Target ACoS below your royalty rate (≈70% at $2.99–$9.99 eBook pricing; less for paperback).
